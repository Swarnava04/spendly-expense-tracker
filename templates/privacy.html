Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with any preferences you set within the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve the Service: displaying your expense history, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery services) strictly to operate the Service, and these providers are bound to handle your data only on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website, and we'll be happy to help.
